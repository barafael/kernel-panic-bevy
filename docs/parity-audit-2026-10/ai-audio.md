# AI opponent and audio parity audit

Which AI a default seat runs upstream: `LuaAI.lua:1-15` offers "Kernel Panic AI" (`KPAI.lua`), "Fair KPAI" (`KPAI_Fair.lua`) and "Invasion AI"; each gadget claims only teams whose `Spring.GetTeamLuaAI(t)` equals its own name (`KPAI.lua:135`, `KPAI_Fair.lua:185`). KP's own skirmish launcher seats the regular one by default: `libs/GenerateGame.lua:292` `skirm.players[1+p].bot=ModSpecific.BotsList[1] -- By default, regular AI`; Fair KPAI ("Easy KP AI (Lua)", `libs/FillModSpecific.lua:58-61`) is used only for the enemy of a "Beginner's Duel" (difficulty 1, `GenerateGame.lua:302-305`) and "Heroic Struggle" (`:309-312`); allies are "always the regular KPAI" (`:301`). The remake ports Fair KPAI for every seat (finding 2).

Hidden information: upstream's AI is a synced gadget and mostly omniscient (UnitCreated marks every enemy minifac spot, `GetUnitsInCylinder` and `GetTeamUnitsByDefs` ignore LOS); only its `GetUnitNearestEnemy` calls are LOS/radar-gated. The remake's AI reads a full snapshot and filters only undetected cloak (finding 9).

## Findings (most important first)

### 1. The AI stops playing once its homebase is dead — severity: high · confidence: verified
- Original: `teamData[t]` is built once per AI team at GameStart (`KPAI_Fair.lua:177-225`) and never removed. Both updates walk every entry, with or without a homebase: fast update `if f%7==0 then for t,td in pairs(teamData) do` → `OrderMiniFac` (`:1103-1117`), slow update `if f % 128 < .1 then … for t,td in pairs(teamData) do` (`:1121-1123`) → missions, `DispatchCon` (spread around the start point `positions[0]`), specials, deploys, `SuicideIfAlone`. A team with only minifacs stays in play in the default mode: `game_over.lua:48` gamemode defaults to 1, `:57` `isLifeSustainerFunc = GameModeKey==1 and isFactoryFunc` (homebase or minifac).
- Remake: the brain only runs teams that own a homebase: `snapshot` pushes into `ai_teams` only `if homebase && is_ai(team.0)` (`units/ai/mod.rs:517-518`), `ai_brain` loops over `s.ai_teams` only (`mod.rs:337-338`), and `run_army` returns at `let home = *homes.first()?;` (`mod.rs:812`). The remake's census keeps such a team alive (`lifecycle/game_over.rs:176` `is_factory()`; test `minifac_keeps_team_alive`, `:442`).
- In play: kill the AI's Kernel/Hole/Carrier while it still owns minifacs and it never issues another order: its army idles where it stands, constructors never claim another vent, Terminal/Obelisk/Firewall never fire, the minifacs keep whatever repeat flag the last tick set (endless uncapped spam piling up at the minifac, or no production at all), `SuicideIfAlone` never runs. Upstream's AI fights on with its minifacs.
- Fix: put every AI seat still in play into `ai_teams`; where `homes` is needed (home radius, defence, spread ring) fall back to the team's start point or nearest own building.

### 2. Every difficulty runs the Fair budget; upstream's default seat is the uncapped "Kernel Panic AI" — severity: high · confidence: verified
- Original: see the preface for the seating. `KPAI.lua` has no budget at all (no `Lack` in the file): homebase `if n*200 < r then` constructor (`KPAI.lua:769`), `if r > 1000 - (forceSize+teamBufferSize)*20` heavy/arty (`:777`), else `for i = 1,3` spam (`:795`); every finished minifac gets `CMD.REPEAT {1}` plus one spam (`:461-475`), so it produces non-stop; `DispatchCon` lacks Fair's `if teamData[t].Lack.buildings<=0` spread branch (`KPAI_Fair.lua:602-610`), so every idle constructor claims a vent; counter-dispatch needs only `teamBufferSize>=3` (`KPAI.lua:897`). Readme: Fair is the one that "limits its growths to avoid outnumbering enemies" (`Kernel_Panic_readme.txt:146`).
- Remake: `Lack::compute(difficulty.fairness_slack(), own, enemy)` gates every order at every difficulty (`mod.rs:360`); head start `8 + 4*slack`, `2 + slack/2`, `1 + slack/4` (`build_orders.rs:107-113`), slack 0/2/4/8 for difficulty 1-4 (`game_setup.rs:567-579`); the default skirmish is a Duel at difficulty 2 (`game_setup.rs:401`, `:95`, `main.rs:254`), so the "Standard Duel" AI may own at most 16 spam, 3 constructors/heavies/arty and 1 building more than its strongest enemy (plus the 4-minifac floor, `expansion.rs:20`). Allied AI seats are capped too. The slack ladder is the remake's documented design, but it is built on Fair, which upstream only uses for its Easy duel.
- In play: in KP a Standard Duel or Team Game AI expands onto every free vent with up to five constructors and floods from every minifac regardless of what the player does; the remake's Normal AI mirrors the player's army and building count with a small head start, so a slow or turtling player meets a far weaker opponent.
- Fix: port `KPAI.lua`'s flavour (Fair minus the `Lack` checks, see `diff KPAI.lua KPAI_Fair.lua`) for difficulty ≥ 2 and for allied seats; keep Fair for difficulty 1 duels.

### 3. Army control is a different model (attack-move pushes, gather-8, base recall, trickle) — severity: medium · confidence: verified
- Original (`KPAI_Fair.lua`, every 128 frames): no defence routine, no gather threshold. `for _=1,1+(td.forceSize+teamBufferSize)*.02` missions (`:1225`) pick a spot by weight (`GetWeightFor`, `:368-385`; enemy vents ≈ 1+force) and size the force (`SelectForceFor`, `:301-347`; an enemy spot gets `spams = 20 + .2 * (td.forceSize+teamBufferSize)` `:324`, 1-2 arty, `1+floor(es/15)` heavies). Spam comes only from own vent spots holding `p.spams > .003 * d` (`:349-366`) and only units standing within 300 of that vent (`GetUnitsInCylinder(td.positions[p].x, td.positions[p].z, 300, t)`, `:1249`); the homebase spot `positions[0]` is not a source (its state is the undefined global `HOMEBASE`, `:191`, which matches none of `:353-363`). Orders: spam `CMD.MOVE` to the spot on a 41-130 ring (`:693-700`), heavies `CMD.MOVE` (`:728`), arty `CMD.ATTACK` on the ground of an enemy spot or `CMD_NX` when crowded (`:715-720`), `CMD.FIGHT` only for arty sent to empty spots (`:714`, `:1164`); the big-army rush is `CMD.ATTACK {k}` on the homebase unit with all free spam, rolled 1 in 5 per update against a random team (`:881-898`, `:1211-1224`).
- Remake: every pushed unit gets `PointOrder::AttackMove` (`mod.rs:876`; `army.rs:147` "stop and engage anything hostile"). An enemy unit within `DEFEND_RADIUS = 700` of a homebase (`mod.rs:98`, `:816-821`) sends every idle army unit on the map there; otherwise units idle within 700 of home wait for `ARMY_THRESHOLD = 8` (`mod.rs:93`, `:857`) while every idle unit outside that radius is pushed on its own each 1 s tick (`mod.rs:862`). Artillery and heavies push with the spam (`army.rs:23-24`).
- In play: KP's waves run past defenders to the target vent shooting on the move, and Pointers/DoS/Flows shell the enemy minifac's ground from range; homebase-built spam stays at the base as a guard until the big rush. In the remake every group stops for every fight on its way, artillery fights whatever it meets instead of shelling the target, one unit poking the AI base pulls its whole idle army home (easy to kite), and units from forward minifacs walk to the front one by one.
- Fix: MOVE for spam/heavies and attack-ground for arty at enemy targets, upstream's per-mission quotas sourced from own vents; drop or document the base recall, gather-8 and field-trickle rules.

### 4. AI constructors never clear Bad Blocks or other blocking features — severity: medium · confidence: verified
- Original: `DispatchCon` first rolls `if math.random(1,2)==1 and ((not lastFeatureBusting[u]) or lastFeatureBusting[u]+900<Spring.GetGameFrame())` (`KPAI_Fair.lua:550`), finds the nearest feature with `fd.blocking and fd.destructable` (`:555-565`), and if `not Spring.GetUnitNearestEnemy(u,math.sqrt(smallest_squared_distance))` (`:567`) builds a Debug within 0-400 of it: `Spring.GiveOrderToUnit(u,-mineblaster,there,{})` (`:571-594`). This runs before the building-budget check, so even a capped AI clears features.
- Remake: `run_constructors` (`mod.rs:648-736`) has no feature step; nothing under `units/ai/` builds the Debug (mineblaster).
- In play: Quad Core, Dual Core, Corrupted Core and Digital Divide place map Bad Blocks and Central Hub its destructible unusable sectors (`units/features.rs:1-4`; `assets/defs/features/unusablesector.tdf` `blocking=1`); KP's AI Debugs them (and any Bad Block wall a player builds) over time, the remake's AI never does, so walls channel or block it for the whole game.
- Fix: port the blast step: idle constructor, 50 % roll, 900-frame per-constructor cooldown, Debug near the nearest blocking feature when no visible enemy is closer than it.

### 5. Mixer limits not modelled: 17 copies per effect, one unit reply at a time — severity: medium · confidence: verified
- Original: every KP effect is an undefined sound item (`gamedata/sounds.lua` `default = {}` is empty), so `maxConcurrent = 16` (`System/Sound/OpenAL/SoundItem.h:64`) applies, tested as `if (maxConcurrent >= currentlyPlaying)` (`SoundItem.cpp:60-66`): at most 17 copies of one effect play at once, further plays are dropped (`SoundSource.cpp:208`). The UnitReply channel has one source and one emit per frame (`Sound.cpp:86-87` `SetMaxConcurrent(1); SetMaxEmits(1);`); a new reply stops the one playing (`AudioChannel.cpp:100-117`, equal priority 0).
- Remake: one global cap, `MAX_SOUNDS = 96` (`audio/mod.rs:374`, `:451-462`), and a dedup of identical consecutive requests (`:456`); no per-effect count, no per-channel limit, so replies overlap.
- In play: a fight of a few dozen Bits/Bugs plays up to 96 overlapping `bitfire` copies (each boosted ×6, `:405`) instead of 17, a much denser and louder din than KP; rapid clicking stacks `select`/`commandgiven` copies where KP cuts the previous reply off.
- Fix: count live sources per `SoundId` (cap 17) and keep one UnitReply source (stop the old one, at most one new per frame).

### 6. Network dispatch: proximity instead of damage, and a different offensive target — severity: low · confidence: verified
- Original: counter-dispatch is in `UnitDamaged` (`KPAI_Fair.lua:943-961`): the teleporter itself must take ≥ 0.5 damage (`:931-933`) from an attacker within `dispatchRange = 300` (`:944`), buffer ≥ 3, `Lack.spams>5`, 5 s per teleporter; target = the attacker's position (`:953`). Offensively each mission makes every Port within 300 of a source vent dispatch at that same vent (`positions[p]`, `:1250-1253`), i.e. packets muster at home and join later missions as spam; Connections never dispatch offensively.
- Remake: every tick each teleporter dispatches at the nearest enemy within 300, any enemy and buildings included (`nearest_enemy(…, COUNTER_DISPATCH_RANGE, |_| true)`, `mod.rs:909-931`), no damage needed; offensively the teleporter nearest the push target (Port or Connection) dispatches at the push target once 12 are buffered (`mod.rs:935-952`).
- In play: an AI Port or Connection spits a 12-packet pulse every 5 s at a unit walking past or at your minifac standing within 300, without being shot; KP's only answers when hit. Offensive packets appear at the forward teleporter and head straight for the front.
- Fix: trigger counter-dispatch from the damage pipeline for teleporter victims, at the attacker; dispatch offensively at the Port's own vent (or keep and document the improvement).

### 7. Artillery never retaliates against enemy heavies — severity: low · confidence: verified
- Original: `UnitDamaged` records every enemy heavy attacker (byte, worm, connection; `isHeavy[aud]`, `KPAI_Fair.lua:937-942`); each slow update the first non-busy arty (pointer/dos/flow) within 2000 that is not in a homebase box gets `Spring.GiveOrderToUnit(u,CMD.ATTACK,{d},{})` and is marked busy (`:1181-1196`).
- Remake: no counterpart; arty only rides the attack-move pushes (`mod.rs:862-877`).
- In play: KP's Pointers/DoS/Flows single out a Byte/Worm/Connection that hits their side; the remake's engage whatever their push meets.
- Fix: record heavy attackers in the damage hook (next to `TeamDamageLog`) and order one idle arty within 2000 to attack each.

### 8. Viruses are never given orders — severity: low · confidence: verified
- Original: viruses are spam (`kpunittypes.lua:100-103`, `:188-199`); an orphan spam is hooked to the closest minifac and a virus is sent there with `CMD.MOVE` (`AddSpam`, `KPAI_Fair.lua:478-495`); missions dispatch it (`:1258` skips only exploits) and the big rush takes it (`:887-888`).
- Remake: the army set is `is_combat_unit() || Connection` (`army.rs:23-24`) and `is_combat_unit` leaves Virus out (`content/definitions.rs:318-332`, test `:558`); viruses still count against the spam budget (`build_orders.rs:52`).
- In play: viruses from Obelisk gas and Worm kills stay where they spawned all game instead of regrouping at a Window and joining attacks, while using up the AI's spam allowance.
- Fix: add Virus to the AI army set (and walk fresh viruses to the nearest own minifac).

### 9. The AI's nearest-enemy checks see through fog — severity: low · confidence: verified
- Original: `Spring.GetUnitNearestEnemy(u, r)` without a third argument tests LOS even in synced code: `wantLOS = !lua_isboolean(L, 3) || …; testLOS = !fullRead || wantLOS` (`Lua/LuaSyncedRead.cpp:3349-3359`) → `Enemy_InLos` (`losStatus[allyteam] & (LOS_INLOS | LOS_INRADAR)`, `Game/GameHelper.cpp:451-456`). KPAI uses it for Bug deploy (`KPAI_Fair.lua:1390`), Exploit undeploy (`:1380`), the Obelisk's Infection target (`:1304`) and the Debug safety check (`:567`).
- Remake: `nearest_enemy` filters allies, dead units and undetected cloak only (`mod.rs:747-768`, `spatial.rs:89-91`); the fog is a view-only pass for the local player (FEATURES.md §24: "The AI never looks at it"). Used for the Obelisk (`mod.rs:1008-1018`), Bug deploy (`:1065`), Exploit undeploy (`:1078`), and the remake-only threat (`:816-821`) and counter-dispatch (`:918-925`) checks. Also every ready Obelisk fires each tick (`:1008`) where KP fires at most one per mission iteration (`break`, `KPAI_Fair.lua:1309`).
- In play: with fog on for the human, AI Obelisks gas units sitting unseen in the fog up to 1500 away and AI Bugs deploy into Exploits against enemies nobody on their side can see.
- Fix: keep a sight mask per AI ally team (the fog pass has the sight rule) and gate those queries on it.

### 10. No "ok" reply for building placement, toggles and several panel commands — severity: low · confidence: verified
- Original: every GUI command goes through `CGuiHandler::GiveCommand` → `selectedUnitsHandler.GiveCommand` (`Game/UI/GuiHandler.cpp:968-981`), which plays the first selected unit's `ok` (`SelectedUnitsHandler.cpp:261-265`): building placement (`GuiHandler.cpp:1301-1311`), `CMDTYPE_ICON` commands such as Stop, Self-destruct, Deploy (`:1342-1350`) and `CMDTYPE_ICON_MODE` toggles such as Repeat and AutoHold (`:1353-1364`).
- Remake: `ack_order` is raised by right-click orders (`selection/right_click.rs:564`), ability casts and the deploy hotkey (`interaction/ability.rs:326`, `:342`, `:367`), Stop (`command_panel/activation.rs:230`) and Produce (`:273`) only. `place_on_release` issues `SimCommand::Build` without it (`ui/hud/placement.rs:665-670`), as do Repeat/AutoHold (`activation.rs:281-304`), panel Deploy/Undeploy (`:305-317`), Enter (`:319-327`), Unset Target (`:328-334`) and Self-destruct (`:335-339`).
- In play: placing a Socket/Window/Port, or toggling Repeat, is silent; KP answers each with `commandgiven`.
- Fix: call `ack_order` at those sites.

### 11. Two GUI beeps never play: FailedCommand and IncomingChat — severity: low · confidence: verified
- Original: `FailedCommand` (`commandfailed.wav`, `gamedata/sounds.lua`) on the UI channel at volume 5 whenever a click yields `CMD_FAILED`, e.g. a building on a blocked site (`GuiHandler.cpp:1303-1306`); `IncomingChat` (`beep4.wav`) at volume 5 for every chat line shown (`Game/Game.cpp:1968-1996`).
- Remake: `SoundId::CommandFailed` and `SoundId::Beep4` have no use outside `audio/mod.rs`; placement hits the failed case and returns silently (`ui/hud/placement.rs:647-650`, `// CMD_FAILED: nothing placed, the command stays armed.`); in-match chat exists (`net/chat.rs`) without a beep.
- In play: clicking an invalid build site gives no audible refusal; a chat line in a networked match arrives silently.
- Fix: push `CommandFailed` (UserInterface, 2D) on armed-click rejections, `Beep4` (2D) when a line is appended to the chat log.

### 12. Selection replies follow "units added", not the click — severity: low · confidence: verified
- Original: a unit click always plays the clicked unit's `select`, including a double-click that grabs every unit of the type and a Ctrl-click deselect (`HandleSingleUnitClickSelection`, `SelectedUnitsHandler.cpp:344-386`); a box plays `select` for one new unit and MultiSelect for more (`:330-340`); recalling a control group is silent (`SelectGroup`, `:476-498`; no sound call in `Game/UI/Groups/`), and so are Lua selections such as `kp_buildbar.lua`'s `Spring.SelectUnitMap` (`:746-753`).
- Remake: `selection_sounds` counts `Added<Selected>`: one → `select`, more → `button9` (`audio/mod.rs:501-517`), whatever added them (group recall `selection/groups.rs:95`, build bar `ui/hud/build_bar.rs:291`).
- In play: every control-group key press beeps or replies; double-clicking a Bit gives the multi-select beep instead of the Bit's reply; a build-bar click plays `select`; Ctrl-click deselect is silent.
- Fix: raise the select sound from the click and box handlers (`selection/core.rs`) with upstream's rule; leave group recall and the build bar silent.

## Water awareness
