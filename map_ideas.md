# Map Ideas

CS-pun names with motherboard/board-game feel. Each tries to make the *terrain shape* match the *concept*, since datavent placement is the only real strategic primitive.

## Stack Overflow (Volcano) — *removed*

Central caldera on a 4-player map; the stack literally overflows. Every 5 minutes the volcano erupts in a ~13 s sequence: bad blocks ring the slope, cloaked logic bombs land further out, a virus swarm boils up to the rim, ICMP packets fan out radially, and one faction-nibble of each *other* faction lands in each enemy base. Intensity scales with eruption count. The map was removed to be remade; `548ad3b` is the last commit with `kernel-panic/assets/maps/Stack_Overflow.kpmap` and its eruption event (`kernel-panic/src/map_events/mod.rs`).

## Stack Overflow (Ziggurat)

Terraced ziggurat. Each ascending tier is a "stack frame" with one datavent. Climbing the stack rewards you with map control but the top is a kill-box once it overflows. Heightmap does most of the work.

## Heap Fragmentation — *implemented* (`heap_fragmentation_v1`, after BAR's Sinkhole Network)

Mostly-flat plain pock-marked with irregular raised "allocated blocks" of varying sizes, separated by void/free-memory channels. Datavents sit on random blocks, never on the floor — turns expansion into a packing problem.

## Race Condition

Strict mirror, two factions, two parallel "threads" (lanes) with a single shared datavent at the join. First side to reach it locks the critical section.

## Deadlock — *the name went to `deadlock_v1`, after BAR's Hooked*

4-player rotational symmetry. Each player's natural expansion path runs through the next player's flank, so nobody can grow without breaking the cycle.

## Null Pointer

Donut. A central uncrossable void (literal `voidGround`) with all datavents on the surrounding ring. Fighting is always over the rim; nobody dereferences the middle.

## Segfault

Plateau split by deep fissures into 4–6 isolated "segments", connected only by narrow ridges. Each segment has 1–2 datavents; control is binary per segment.

## Cache Miss

Concentric L1/L2/L3 rings. Inner ring datavents are close and cheap; outer ring datavents are far but plentiful. Players choose latency vs. throughput.

## Circular Buffer — *implemented*

Pure ring corridor, no interior. Players spawn at evenly-spaced indices and read/write around the ring. Wrapping around behind an enemy is the entire game.

Implementation: one map per player count, `circular_buffer_{3,4,5,6}_v1`: a regular n-gon corridor round a void core, a start pocket off the middle of every edge, kernel pillars at the corners leaving a passage either side (a slight chokepoint). Two directional fast lanes run round the ring — the inner one clockwise, the outer one counter-clockwise: along a lane's direction travel runs at 1.6×, against it 0.4×, off the lanes unchanged ([`spring_map::ring`](spring-map/src/ring.rs), [`LaneFlow`](kernel-panic/src/map_events/lane_flow.rs)). Generated by `spring-map/examples/gen_circular_buffer.rs`; the first, square Circular Buffer with a whole-map clockwise swirl is in the git history.

## Pipeline — *implemented* (`pipeline_v1`, after BAR's SpeedMetal)

Long, narrow rectangular map (e.g. 6×16) divided into 4–5 stages by gentle ridges. Datavents one-per-stage. Forces front-line progression rather than flanking.

## Endianness

Two-player mirror where one half's heightmap is bit-reversed relative to the other — high ground on the left maps to low ground on the right. Reads symmetric on the minimap but plays asymmetric.

## Heat Sink — *implemented* (`heat_sink_v1`)

Radial fins. A central CPU die with finned ridges radiating outward; datavents in the troughs between fins. Kbots love the fins, tanks live in the troughs.

Implementation: straight edges only. The four banks of fins run along the axes (four fins and three troughs a bank, a vent halfway down each trough, a bus lane, the heat pipe, down the middle one), the die is a raised blue plateau with a ramp on each side, and a start plateau with a second vent and a mounting hole (void) sits in each corner; quarter-turn symmetric, 4096 elmos. Every mover has the same slope limit here, so "kbots on the fins" did not carry over: the fins are walls.

## Fork Bomb — *implemented* (`fork_bomb_v1`)

8-player FFA. Tiny starting alcoves around the rim, each with one datavent, all feeding into a chaotic open center with 2–3 contested vents. Designed to die fast and loud.

Implementation: two alcoves a side, each a stem that splits in a bar into two tines (the fork) running into one open arena over the void. Five contested vents: one at the middle of each side, between the two forks that reach it, and one on a plateau in the middle; four mesas for cover. Quarter-turn symmetric, 3072 elmos, every alcove the same walk from the middle.

## Boot Sector — *implemented* (`boot_sector_v1`)

Deliberately tiny (4×4) duel map. Two datavents per side, one in the middle. The KP equivalent of a chess opening trainer.

Implementation: 2048 elmos drawn as a chessboard (one colour per square, all one height), a home and a second vent a side, the third vent on a plateau between whose two ramps face the homes, four mesas at its corners. Half-turn symmetric: the homes walk the same to the middle.
